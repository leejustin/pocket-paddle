Pocket Pickle sounds

All bundled sounds (assets/sounds/*.wav) are original, synthesized by
scripts/generate_sounds.py. No third-party recording is included in the app.

The synthesis was tuned against measurements of "Pickleball Lincoln Nebraska.mp3"
by bmccoy2 (CC BY 4.0, https://freesound.org/people/bmccoy2/sounds/477653/).
That recording is not distributed with the app.
