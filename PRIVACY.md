# Pocket Pickle Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Pocket Pickle is a toy that turns your phone into a pickleball paddle. It is built to collect as little as possible.

## What stays on your phone

- **Motion.** The app reads your phone's motion sensors to detect swings. That data is processed on the phone and never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Settings.** Your paddle face, sound and swing sensitivity are saved on the phone only.
- **Paddle photo.** If you choose a photo for the paddle face, the app copies that one photo into its own private storage on the phone. It never uploads it and never reads the rest of your photo library.

## Rally with a friend

When you choose **Find a friend nearby**, your phone looks for another Pocket Pickle phone on the same Wi-Fi (or, between two iPhones, directly). During a rally the two phones exchange only game information, such as when the ball was hit and the score, and a random name like "Paddle 4821". Nothing goes through a server or over the internet, and nothing is kept after the rally ends.

## Purchases

Pocket Pickle Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through the App Store. To confirm the purchase and unlock Pro, the app uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), which receives an anonymous customer ID and your purchase history for this app. This is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No ads and no advertising identifiers
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No microphone or camera access
- No selling or sharing of personal information

## Children

Pocket Pickle is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
