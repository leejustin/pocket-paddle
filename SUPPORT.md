# Pocket Pickle Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/leejustin/pocket-pickle/issues/new) and describe what happened, including your phone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**The paddle doesn't make a sound.** Turn up the volume (the app plays even in silent mode), and check that Pocket Pickle has Motion & Fitness access in Settings → Pocket Pickle.

**Swings don't register, or register too easily.** Open the ••• menu and change **Swing sensitivity** (Chill, Normal or Twitchy).

**I can't find my friend's phone for a rally.** Both phones need Pocket Pickle open on the **Rally with a friend** screen, on the same Wi-Fi (two iPhones can also connect directly). If it says Local Network is off, turn it on in Settings → Pocket Pickle.

**I bought Pro on another phone.** Open the ••• menu, tap the Pocket Pickle Pro card, and choose **Restore purchase** while signed in with the same Apple ID.

**Safety.** Hold on tight and give yourself room. A wrist strap or a case with a grip helps.
